03 • THE BUILD'S ACCELERATOR

Five-Stage Registry Accelerator

01

MATCH

Customer need → participating program → appropriate Affiliate.

02

CONNECT

Customer ↔ Affiliate ↔ Program Owner through the Registry's contact pathways.

03

TRANSACT

The customer uses the payment method offered by the participating Program Owner.

04

CONFIRM

The participating Program Owner confirms qualifying referrals or transactions under its own rules.

05

REWARD

The participating Program Owner calculates and pays applicable Affiliate compensation according to its disclosed terms.

No marketplace API is required for the basic pipeline. The basic Registry pathway can facilitate referral attribution, communication, payment-routing information, and commission reporting without requiring CJ, Shopify, eBay, or another marketplace API.

What the Registry does

  • Provides discovery and connection pathways.
  • Displays participating businesses and programs.
  • Provides referral/contact routes supplied by participating programs.
  • Provides educational and explainer material.
  • Links visitors to external services where appropriate.

What the Registry does not promise

  • No guaranteed sales.
  • No guaranteed Affiliate income.
  • No guaranteed commission.
  • No guaranteed product result, delivery, refund, or payment outcome.
  • No control over third-party payment networks.
04 • PAYMENT BOUNDARY

Customer Payment Choice

Customers may pay using the payment portal or payment method offered by the participating Program Owner.

Depending on the program and provider availability, options may include Orange Money / Max it, Sendwave-supported mobile-wallet transfers, Cash App, or another designated payment method.

The Registry does not request customer PINs, passwords, or private banking credentials and does not represent that it controls third-party payment networks.

Safety: Verify the participating business, recipient, amount, and payment instructions before sending money. Never provide passwords, PINs, or private banking credentials to an Affiliate.
